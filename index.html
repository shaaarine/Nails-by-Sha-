I started doing nails in May 2025 after discovering Russian Manicure while looking for a healthier and more sustainable way to care for my natural nails. What started as something I wanted to learn for myself eventually became something I truly love, from creating clean, healthy-looking nails to bringing creative nail designs to life ✧ When you book with me, you can expect a gentle, detailed, and personalized experience, and I’d always love to hear your ideas and try something new together ♡

Policies & guidelines

A few things that keep the schedule fair for everyone — thanks for understanding.

Deposits

A ₱500 deposit is required to confirm your slot; it's deducted from your total. After booking, send a screenshot of your deposit payment to @sharine.nails on IG via DM — your slot isn't fully confirmed until proof of payment is received. Squeeze-in appointments (outside regular open slots) may be accommodated when possible — message @sharine.nails on IG to ask.

Cancellations

Please give 24–48 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule — late cancellations may forfeit the deposit.

Late arrivals

A 15-minute grace period applies. Beyond that: 16–20 minutes late is a ₱300 late fee, 21–30 minutes late is a ₱500 late fee. Around 30 minutes to 1 hour late, the appointment may need to be rescheduled to protect the next client's slot.

Home service

Available around Parañaque City and nearby cities around Metro Manila. Add ₱500 on top of the service price for home service; transportation is shouldered by the client (Grab/InDrive fare to and from location OR pickup at my location — whichever's more convenient for you).

Send your design or nail inspo photos via DM to @sharine.nails on IG, 24–48 hours before your appointment. Not sure what something costs? Send the photo and a quote will be provided.
