Policies & guidelines

A few things that keep the schedule fair for everyone — thanks for understanding.

Booking & deposits

A deposit is required to confirm your slot; it's deducted from your total. Squeeze-in appointments (outside regular open slots) may be accommodated when possible — message @sharine.nails to ask.

Cancellations & late arrivals

Please give as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule — late cancellations may forfeit the deposit. A grace period applies for late arrivals; beyond that, the appointment may need to be rescheduled to protect the next client's slot.

Home service

Available around Parañaque City and nearby. Add ₱500 on top of the service price for home service; transportation is shouldered by the client (fare, pickup, or padala — whichever's easiest for you).
